# Glue Kitchen Guide

Phone app for the glue kitchen operator in a particleboard plant (Dieffenbacher ProGuide gluing line).

**Open / install:** https://wassimgo73-blip.github.io/glue-kitchen-guide/

## What's inside
- **Calculators** – full SL/CL layer recipe (resin, hardener, wax emulsion, urea, extra water, glued-chip moisture), actual resin factor check, flow converter (l/min · kg/min · t/h), silo volume and time left, resin use from production.
- **Faults** – 27 common faults with causes, actions and safety notes, searchable and filtered by area (glue kitchen, glue silos, chip silos/bins, blender, HMI, board quality).
- **Areas** – what each section does and what to watch.
- **Reference** – typical values and core formulas.
- **Shift check** – start-of-shift checklist, saved on the phone.
- **Glossary** – English / French / Arabic.

Works offline after the first visit. Your inputs are stored only on your phone.

## Install on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → tap **Install** in the app, or menu ⋮ → **Install app / Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

> Pre-filled values are typical examples. Always use your plant's recipe and the supplier's data sheet.
